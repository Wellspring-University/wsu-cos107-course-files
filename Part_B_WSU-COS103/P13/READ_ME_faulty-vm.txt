Practical 13 Part B uses a virtual machine image called faulty-vm.qcow2 with two injected software faults.

It is not included here because it is several gigabytes and must be built on your own lab image.
Build it by following the Instructor Companion, section "Building faulty-vm.qcow2 for Practical 13"
(start from a clean Practical 11-style Ubuntu VM, inject two of faults S1 to S4, then take the snapshot 'issued').

Place the finished file on the lab share. Students copy it into ~/COS107/vm as described in Practical 13.
